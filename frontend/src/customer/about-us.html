About Glow & Grace

A calm salon space for simple, confident beauty.

Glow & Grace is a modern salon studio for hair, skin, makeup, nails, and bridal care. Our goal is to make every appointment feel organized, friendly, and easy to book.

Our story

Beauty service without confusion.

We created Glow & Grace around one simple idea: customers should know what service they are booking, who will help them, how long it will take, and what it will cost.

From quick grooming to bridal appointments, the salon experience should feel clear, respectful, and relaxed from the first click to the final look.
